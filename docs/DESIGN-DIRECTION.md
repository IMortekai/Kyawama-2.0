# Design direction — practical learning, human opportunity

Proposal for the first homepage. Motion direction is inferred from Kris's request and Steve's response to Ruvixx; specific effects remain open to review.

## Overall character
Warm, capable and optimistic. Give the people and programme room to breathe. Retain green, cream and terracotta, with a stronger editorial rhythm and a clear visual thread from first digital steps to professional readiness.

## Recommended composition
- Cream header with retained wordmark, a few clear links and one support action.
- Green hero. Large short headline on the left; approved photography or a purposeful learning-path composition on the right. Desktop balance about 55/45; stack copy before visual on mobile.
- Cream people/story section so the organisation feels local and tangible.
- A three-stage learning journey, connected visually but readable in normal document order.
- A strong pilot panel displaying the planned cohort, duration and weekly schedule.
- Support options and direct contact with a clear distinction between funding, programme and referral questions.

Use wide editorial rows, image/text pairs and occasional panels. Avoid a page composed entirely of identical cards. Keep narrative density under control: one point per section and short labels on the process visual.

## Signature visual
An open curved path connecting three stations: digital basics, professional practice, next opportunities. The path continues beyond the last station to suggest possibilities rather than guaranteed placement. On first entry, the line and station markers can appear in sequence. Keep visible labels and a complete static state.

The existing site already uses an abstract path with coloured circles. Develop that recognisable idea rather than importing unrelated AI-dashboard graphics. A person-led hero is the preferred alternative if a suitable approved photo is supplied.

## Layout starting values
Content width around 1160–1240 px; desktop side padding around 32–48 px, mobile 20–24 px. Comfortable body text around 17–19 px; compact eyebrow labels with restrained tracking. Give headlines short line lengths and deliberate wraps. Use fluid scales rather than desktop values compressed onto a phone.

## Review questions for the first screenshot
Can I identify Kyawama and its programme without scrolling? Does the primary action make sense for a supporter? Does the design suggest people learning and working? Are pilot figures clearly future plans? Does each section add something? Is the motion noticeable because it helps the story?

Do not import GoManzanas's gold/white consulting identity, Ruvixx's service taxonomy or a template's copy. The actual Ruvixx demo must be supplied before anyone claims to match its exact interaction language.
