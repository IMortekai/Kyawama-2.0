# Homepage specification

Use `content/homepage-draft.json` for draft strings. This document owns layout, meaning and behaviour. F-identifiers refer to `SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md`.

## 1. Header
Cream background; original wordmark when supplied, otherwise provisional plain text. Anchor links to story, programme and support. Support action has strongest emphasis. Accessible mobile menu with clear expanded state, keyboard focus and Escape behaviour if implemented as an overlay. Sticky is optional; it must not obscure anchors.

## 2. Hero — understand the mission
Eyebrow: rural Zambia / digital skills / remote-work readiness. Proposed headline: **Digital skills. A fairer start.** One sentence explains adults, practical learning and preparation for remote opportunities (F02/F03/F08). Primary action: Support the pilot → `#support`. Secondary: Explore the programme → `#programme`.

Visual: approved teaching/person image, or the abstract learning path described in DESIGN-DIRECTION.md. This area should carry the main visual energy. No rotating messages, autoplay video, forced loading sequence or animated headline that takes several seconds to become readable.

Keep the October date out of the hero for now; show it as a planned detail in the pilot section instead.

## 3. People and origin — explain why this exists (`#story`)
A compact editorial story connects Steve's time in Zambia with Fanny's progression into remote work (F10/F12). Explain that her experience helped shape the programme. Do not call her a graduate of an already completed Kyawama cohort.

If approved portraits exist, use Fanny prominently and identify her correctly. If none exist, use a strong text-led section; do not fill it with unrelated stock faces. Include a small team line naming Steve, Fanny and Raeesa with their public roles (F09–F11). Detailed biographies belong on About later.

## 4. Learning journey — make the programme tangible (`#programme`)
Three stages with meaningful progression:
1. **Build digital confidence** — everyday computer tasks, email, documents and online safety.
2. **Practise working professionally** — communication, collaboration and remote-team expectations.
3. **Prepare for opportunities** — CVs, applications, interviews and credible opportunity searches.

Connect the stages visually on desktop; use a vertical flow on mobile. This is a description of the programme, not a claim that every learner gets a job. Include a plain sentence that employment is not guaranteed. Do not use the current Get placed label as a promise.

## 5. Pilot — show a specific plan (`#pilot`)
Use the heading **The first pilot, built around practice.** Display planned 15 learners / 16 weeks / eight scheduled hours each week, plus the current planned October 2026 start (F04–F06). The note should make clear that the venue is being confirmed.

Explain six lesson hours plus two supervised practice hours. Values stay static; no counting-up animation that resembles achieved impact. Store these details centrally and flag them in the reviewer notes as requiring confirmation before publication.

## 6. Support — give each visitor a useful role (`#support`)
Introduce the needs of the pilot without claiming an unverified current funding balance. Present four primary routes: funding, equipment/connectivity, venue support, teaching/mentoring. A short additional line covers work opportunities and learner referrals. No invented sponsorship tiers, prices, donation goal or payment widget.

Primary CTA: Talk about supporting the pilot → Steve's public email. Secondary CTA: Programme and volunteering enquiries → Raeesa's public email. Do not send messages automatically.

Include a compact FAQ: whether the course is online (classroom delivery), whether computer experience is required (no), whether a job is guaranteed (no), and how to help (contact by contribution type). Reserve detailed legal/financial answers for approved FAQ content.

## 7. Contact/footer (`#contact`)
Clear names, public roles and public email links (F17), useful section links, full public brand and current copyright year. Don't add registered-charity badges, numbers, tax statements, a made-up address or placeholder social links.

## Optional only after clarification
A small learning-materials/Ace1t explanation can live on the later Programme page once public messaging and rollout are confirmed. Keep it disabled in the initial draft. Do not turn the homepage into an Ace1t sales page or show a dead learner-login button.

## Content not required for this pass
External macro-statistics, impact counters, testimonials, news feed, supporter-logo strip and long founder biography. Add them only when they earn their space and the required evidence/assets exist.
