# Content rules

## Authority by subject
1. Current explicit instructions/approvals from Kris, Steve or Raeesa control the project and approved organisational claims.
2. Current public pages supply the public baseline: programme detail, brand, contact and published story. Time-sensitive and legal details still need a launch check.
3. The supplied internal capture carries significant weight for intended direction and the Ace1t work, with its original caveats. It does not automatically authorise publishing internal plans.
4. Prior chats guide process and design preferences, not Kyawama facts.
5. Proposed copy is editorial interpretation and remains editable.

When sources conflict, record it. Don't resolve a conflict just by preferring the longest or newest summary. Missing information in one source is not a contradiction.

## Voice
Use clear, specific language about learning, opportunity and practical support. Use people-first wording and preserve the agency of learners and local staff. Avoid rescue narratives, pity-led imagery, exaggerated transformation claims and generic charity slogans.

Use British English in newly drafted body copy (programme, organisation, practise as a verb). Retain supplied official role titles and proper names exactly unless the organisation requests a change. Keep Ace1t's spelling.

## Claims to handle carefully
- Free training does not mean guaranteed jobs, placements or income.
- Remote-work preparation does not mean the programme is delivered remotely.
- Pilot targets are plans, not impact totals.
- Fanny's experience is an origin story, not a completed-cohort testimonial.
- Ruvixx contributes expertise according to the public site; do not invent a financial partnership or logo endorsement.
- No public Ace1t launch, account workflow, access link or platform feature is confirmed by the supplied notes.
- The FAQ describes nonprofit establishment as pending. Do not add tax-deductibility wording or registered-charity badges.
- Published no-sponsor/no-cohort statements can become stale. Confirm them before reuse at launch.

## Internal information
The raw capture includes staff commentary, commercial context, wiki IDs, personal account information and unrelated Ruvixx details. None belongs in public copy, source bundles or browser data. Do not deploy this package as a directory or expose its raw documents through the app.

## Source traceability
The draft JSON includes claim IDs and status fields for editorial work. Only map its public strings into components. Keep review status and source notes in developer documentation, not visible in the visitor journey. Review builds can show one unobtrusive draft indicator outside the final production UI.

No empty `[TBD]` claims, lorem ipsum, fabricated statistics or fake success states in a client-facing preview. Omit unavailable sections or use a deliberate complete fallback. Report unresolved items in the handoff.
