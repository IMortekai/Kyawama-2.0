# Master brief

Version 0.1 · 23 September 2026 · Proposal, not final client sign-off.

## Purpose
Give Kyawama a stronger public presence that explains its work, builds trust in the first Zambia pilot and turns interest into useful conversations. The founder liked Kris's Ruvixx demo; Kris proposes bringing similar polish and purposeful movement to this site.

## Recommended audience order
1. Prospective founding sponsors and funding partners.
2. Equipment, venue, teaching, mentoring and employment collaborators.
3. Community organisations and people who can recommend learners.
4. Learners and instructors seeking programme information.

This order follows the current site's emphasis; Steve may change it. The website is not the only recruitment route: the programme reaches adults who may not be online.

## Message
Kyawama is preparing adults in underserved Zambian communities for practical digital work. The planned pilot combines classroom digital literacy with professional preparation and support in pursuing remote opportunities. Its model depends on patient teaching, local leadership and useful partnerships. Employment is not guaranteed.

Proposed narrative: **the opportunity → the people → the learning journey → the pilot → how to help**.

## Workstreams
| Workstream | Treatment in this website project |
|---|---|
| Public website redesign | Current scope: first homepage concept, then supporting pages after review |
| Curriculum move to Ace1t | Relevant internal context; not a request to rebuild the learning platform |
| New founder-requested features | Record in open questions; no confirmed list yet |
| Publishing and operation | Hosting, ownership and update workflow remain to be decided |

## First deliverable
A responsive homepage with proposed copy, working in-page links and public email CTAs, the observed Kyawama palette, a distinctive learning-journey visual, intentional light motion and reduced-motion support. Use approved real photography if available; otherwise make the abstract version feel complete.

The first pass should demonstrate design quality and an intelligible story. It should not require a CMS, login system, payment processor, database, contact-form backend or Ace1t integration.

## Later pages
About, Programme, Support and FAQ. Preserve their substantive current content while making navigation and content hierarchy clearer. Add stories/updates only when there are real materials and someone to maintain them.

## Success criteria
- A visitor can quickly understand who the programme serves, how it works, its pilot status and how to help.
- The homepage visibly expresses Kyawama's own identity and local programme context.
- Sponsorship is a clear enquiry path; no donation checkout is implied.
- Pilot plans are not presented as delivered outcomes.
- Motion helps attention and comprehension without slowing access to content.
- The mobile version receives the same design attention as desktop.
- Every substantial claim and missing asset has a traceable source or an explicit open item.

## Prior-project lessons applied

Retain the Ruvixx lessons about rhythm and motion, and the GoManzanas instruction against scrolljacking. Do not transfer GoManzanas's service copy, gold palette or its deployment arrangements to Kyawama.
