# Current site audit

Source: https://kyawama.org/ · inspected 23 September 2026. Read-only review of all five rendered views, desktop presentation and FAQ DOM content. This is a concise factual inventory and editorial assessment, not a full crawl/export or an independent fact check.

## Method and limits
Search located the site but returned a JavaScript loading shell. A live browser rendered it. Home, About, Program, Support and FAQ were opened through visible navigation. They retained the root address during inspection. Public page source was also inspected for existing styles. No forms were submitted and no emails were sent.

Only one desktop screenshot is retained. Mobile behaviour, measured performance, search indexing, inbox delivery and every interaction were not comprehensively tested. No additional site routes or documents were discovered in the inspected navigation. This does not prove none exist.

## Keep
- The accessible, concrete programme proposition and free-course emphasis.
- The people and origin story, with Fanny's local role visible.
- Clear boundaries around employment expectations.
- Multiple ways to support the work, including referrals for people who are offline.
- Green/cream/terracotta brand, strong typography and the existing path/circle motif.

## Improve
- Make the human story and learning process visually central; the inspected site is text-led, with no photo assets identified in the rendered views.
- Reduce repeated narrative and create a more deliberate sequence from purpose to support.
- Replace the ambiguous placement-stage label with language about preparing and pursuing opportunities.
- Distinguish planned pilot figures from results. Give time-sensitive content one central source.
- Give later pages genuine URLs. Existing navigation is represented as clickable generic elements in the accessibility snapshot; implement semantic links/buttons and test keyboard operation.
- Add meaningful page titles and metadata in the new implementation. The rendered document title was empty in the inspected browser, while the fetched loading shell had a generic title. Search visibility was not measured.
- Keep current legal status and funding wording current rather than expanding them into unverified claims.

## Context figures
Home displays 36% Africa internet use versus 74% worldwide for 2025, a 230 million digital-skills jobs figure for Sub-Saharan Africa by 2030, and a regional urban/rural-gap statement. The footer names ITU and IFC without a precise linked source in the inspected view. These figures are recorded for review, not independently validated here. Omit them from the default first draft; retrieve original reports and verify geography, year, denominator and forecast wording if retained.
